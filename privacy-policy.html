Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy is a plain-language summary of how Noir Corset handles the information you share with us. It is provided as a general template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer — if you operate in a jurisdiction with specific data protection requirements (such as GDPR in the EU/UK), please have this reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, start a custom build, or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly — typically your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and city. We do not collect payment card details, since all payments are handled as Cash on Delivery.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to process and deliver your order, respond to questions, and — only if you opt in through our newsletter form — to send you updates about new collections and custom-build openings. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

How Orders Are Processed

All orders and custom build requests are submitted to us directly via WhatsApp. This means the information you enter in our forms is sent through WhatsApp's platform (operated by Meta) to reach us — please refer to WhatsApp's own privacy policy for how they handle messages sent through their service.

Local Storage & Cookies

Our wishlist feature stores your saved items directly in your browser's local storage — this data stays on your device and is not sent to us or any third party unless you choose to complete an order. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

Analytics

We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to understand overall site traffic and which pages are most useful to visitors. Umami is cookieless and does not track individual visitors across other websites or build advertising profiles — it only reports anonymous, aggregate statistics like page views and general visit counts.

Third-Party Services

Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, where enabled, uses Google's Gemini API to power our on-site shopping assistant. Messages you send to the assistant are processed by Google to generate a response; we do not store your chat history on our own servers.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it — just message us on WhatsApp or email us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our business or tools change. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to noircorset3@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.
